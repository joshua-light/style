Design system · draft 1

Raid performance report

Six sizes, seven spacings, one border. Dark gray surfaces, black ink, a tan accent, Melli fills.

Text

Body text runs at 16px on a 24px line and never wider than 640px. Headings and titles wear the black outline that ElvUI draws around its text; running text stays plain. Extra-bold marks emphasis; nothing is italic.

A second paragraph sits 16px below the first. Blocks of other kinds sit 24px apart.

Lists

  • Lists use square markers.
  • Items sit 8px apart.
    • A nested list indents one step.
    • It keeps the same marker.
      • Three levels is the limit.
  • The list picks up again after a nested one.
  1. Numbered lists are for steps and ranks.
  2. Numbers are dim, not faint: they are read.
    1. Nested steps use letters.
    2. They indent one step.
  3. The count picks up again after a nested list.
